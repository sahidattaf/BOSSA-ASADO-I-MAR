# BOSSA-ASADO-I-MAR

BOSSA-ASADO-I-MAR is a developing restaurant concept bringing South American flavors to the waterfront in Willemstad. The first restaurant will open across from the Avilla Hotel, serving grilled specialties and fresh seafood in a laid-back atmosphere with live music and beachside views.

## Custom GPT Lineup
This project includes fifteen custom GPTs that support every aspect of the business:

1. **Menu Planner GPT** – experiments with new dishes and seasonal menus.
2. **Recipe GPT** – provides step‑by‑step instructions for each menu item.
3. **Wine Pairing GPT** – suggests ideal wine matches for any meal.
4. **Nutrition GPT** – analyzes dishes for dietary and allergen information.
5. **Booking GPT** – handles table reservations and private events.
6. **Hostess GPT** – greets guests and manages seating arrangements.
7. **Sommelier GPT** – offers details about available wines and spirits.
8. **Chef’s Assistant GPT** – helps kitchen staff with prep and cooking times.
9. **Supply Chain GPT** – tracks inventory and orders ingredients.
10. **Training GPT** – onboards new staff with interactive lessons.
11. **Marketing GPT** – creates social media posts and promotional content.
12. **Customer Support GPT** – responds to guest feedback and inquiries.
13. **Event Planner GPT** – designs in‑house events and themed nights.
14. **Analytics GPT** – compiles sales data to guide business decisions.
15. **Community Engagement GPT** – organizes outreach and partnership initiatives.
