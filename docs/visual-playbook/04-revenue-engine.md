## Purpose
Show how BOSSA earns revenue across daily food sales, weekend boxes, party orders, drinks, specials, and future Stripe-enabled preorders.

## Key message
BOSSA revenue should not depend on one product. It compounds through boxes, skewers, sandwiches, sides, drinks, parties, specials, and repeat customers.

1. Content creates attention
2. WhatsApp captures demand
3. Weekend boxes convert fast
4. Sides/drinks raise ticket size
5. Party orders increase volume
6. Notion/KPI review improves next week
