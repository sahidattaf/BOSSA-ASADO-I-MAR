## Purpose
Map the founder skills needed to operate BOSSA’s restaurant, AI, marketing, website, Notion, Stripe, and customer flow.

3. Fire Operations
4. WhatsApp Sales
5. Visual Marketing
6. Notion Control
7. Stripe Payments
8. KPI Review
