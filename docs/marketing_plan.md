# Marketing and Research Plan

1. **Market Research**
   - Analyze demographics of target locations with high urban foot traffic.
   - Survey potential customers on preferred dining experiences and pricing expectations.
   - Monitor competitor restaurants that focus on Latin American cuisine.

2. **Branding Strategy**
   - Develop a visual identity featuring warm colors and modern typography.
   - Showcase the fusion of Brazilian and Argentinian influences in all promotional materials.
   - Maintain active social media channels with engaging food photography and behind-the-scenes content.

3. **Launch Campaign**
   - Host a grand opening event with tasting portions and live music.
   - Partner with local influencers to spread the word online.
   - Offer limited-time discounts or loyalty programs for early customers.

4. **Long-term Growth**
   - Evaluate customer feedback to refine the menu and service.
   - Explore pop-up opportunities and event catering to increase brand exposure.
   - Consider collaborations with beverage brands or local artists.
