# Example GPT Prompts

The following are sample prompts to assist with brainstorming menu items, marketing slogans, or customer engagement strategies using a language model.

## Menu Brainstorm
```
You are a creative chef who combines Brazilian and Argentinian flavors. Suggest five innovative appetizer ideas that highlight grilled seafood and seasonal produce.
```

## Marketing Slogan
```
Generate three catchy slogans for a restaurant that specializes in South American grilling with a modern twist. Keep each slogan under 10 words.
```

## Customer Engagement Social Post
```
Write a friendly social media post inviting food enthusiasts to our upcoming tasting event. Mention our signature cocktails and grilled seafood specials.
```
