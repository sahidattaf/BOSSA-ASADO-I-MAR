# BOSSA Video KPI Scorecard

## North-star metrics

The engine is designed to create measurable business outcomes, not vanity content.

| Objective | Primary KPI | Supporting KPIs |
|---|---|---|
| Reservations | Confirmed reservations attributed | Link clicks, inquiries, conversion rate |
| Catering | Qualified catering leads | WhatsApp clicks, proposal requests, revenue |
| Private events | Event inquiries | Discovery calls, proposals, booked value |
| Tourist awareness | Restaurant visits or bookings | Reach, completion rate, profile visits |
| Staff training | Training completion | Quiz score, incident reduction, manager sign-off |

## Required measurement windows
- 24 hours: delivery and early creative signal
- 7 days: campaign conversion signal
- 30 days: revenue and reusable-learning signal

## Core formulas

```text
Hook Rate = 3-second viewers / video starts
Completion Rate = completed views / video starts
Click-through Rate = destination clicks / impressions
Inquiry Conversion = qualified inquiries / destination clicks
Booking Conversion = confirmed bookings / qualified inquiries
Revenue per Video = attributed revenue / published videos
```

## Decision rules

- High reach + low completion: replace the opening or shorten the video.
- High completion + low clicks: strengthen the CTA and destination offer.
- High clicks + low inquiries: fix landing page, WhatsApp flow, or offer clarity.
- High inquiries + low bookings: route to Sales Operator and improve follow-up.
- Strong conversion: localize into additional approved languages and reuse the format.
