# BOSSA Video Approval Checklist

## Commercial accuracy
- Offer matches the approved campaign brief.
- Price is verified or omitted.
- Dates and availability are verified or omitted.
- Opening hours and location details are verified or omitted.
- No unsupported awards, reviews, guarantees, or health claims appear.

## Language and delivery
- Language is correct for the target audience.
- Pronunciation of BOSSA, Curaçao, people, and menu items is approved.
- Spoken delivery sounds natural.
- Subtitle spelling and timing are correct.
- Subtitle text remains inside safe margins.

## Brand and visual quality
- Correct logo and brand assets are used.
- Food imagery represents the actual offer.
- Avatar use is authorized.
- Music and visual assets are licensed or approved.
- Video format matches the destination channel.

## Conversion
- The video contains one primary CTA.
- CTA wording matches the campaign objective.
- WhatsApp, reservation, or landing-page link has been tested.
- Tracking identifier is attached.
